      FAQ

      Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

      No. This build is a front-end mock — the file is read in your browser tab and thrown away when you close it.

      Why does the same file always score the same?

      The score is derived from a hash of the file's name and size, so the demo is stable and repeatable rather than random every refresh.

      What's the score actually measuring?

      Coverage of the skills a reviewer expects for a junior/mid role, weighted by how many of the keywords show up in your document.
